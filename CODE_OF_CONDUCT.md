# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want GrowLab to be a useful and respectful open source project.

Participants are expected to communicate with clarity, patience and respect. Technical disagreement is welcome when it stays focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Respect maintainers' time and review boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information.
- Abusive or discriminatory language.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Pushing unsafe physical-control changes without review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.
